# Kakeibo: design decisions

These decisions were agreed before any code was written. Update this file when a decision changes.

## Platform and install

- Native SwiftUI + SwiftData. The minimum iOS version is 18, so the app builds with Xcode 26 or 27.
- The owner's Mac (13-inch Intel MacBook Pro 2019) can't run the Xcode needed for an iOS 27.2 iPhone.
  The app is therefore **built on GitHub Actions** and installed from a **Windows PC with iLoader on a free
  Apple ID**, which means a refresh every 7 days. (v1 planned AltStore on the Mac; the owner uses iLoader.)
  - Why not a paid account: about ¥12,800 a year for one budget app works against the point of budgeting.
    Revisit this when iCloud or sharing is wanted.
- Data lives in the app's own container, not the App Group, so it survives refreshes and App Group ID
  rewrites by the installer. The App Group is used only for the widget snapshot and for quick entries
  (the published list and widget taps waiting to be saved; see Quick entries). A free Apple ID can't
  use our group name, so the sideloader registers its own and signs the app and widget with it. `AppGroup`
  finds it at launch: AltStore's `ALTAppGroups` Info.plist key if present, else the groups in the bundle's
  `embedded.mobileprovision` (iLoader registers `group.com.matisse.kakeibo.<team ID>` and writes no
  Info.plist key), else `group.com.matisse.kakeibo`. Asking for a group the app isn't signed with gives a
  private store, which left every widget on placeholders until this was fixed.
- Installing a new build over the old one must keep the data, so SwiftData model changes are additive
  only: new stored properties with defaults, never renamed or removed without a migration. Preferences
  that aren't data (grouping, Home layout) live in `UserDefaults` (`SettingsKey`).
- The project is generated by XcodeGen. CI runs the tests and builds an unsigned, ad-hoc-entitled IPA.

## Accounts

- There are four types:
  - **Bank:** SMBC bank.
  - **Top-up:** Revolut, PayPay and PASMO.
  - **Cash.**
  - **Credit:** SMBC Olive credit, Rakuten Card, Paidy.
- Every account has a balance: a starting balance plus every entry. An **Adjust** entry corrects drift
  and never counts as spending.
- **Top-up** accounts have a setting: "count top-ups as spending in [category]".
  - PASMO: on, counted as Transport. No balance and no ride logging.
  - Revolut and PayPay: off. Each purchase is logged on its own.
- **Credit** accounts store a closing day (締め日), a payment day (支払日), the payment month (same
  month, next month (the usual) or the month after next), what happens when the payment day is a weekend
  or a Japanese bank holiday (next business day by default, previous business day, or keep the date), the
  account the bill is paid from, an optional credit limit, a colour (Blue, Teal, Raspberry, Amber, Violet,
  Graphite) and two alert switches (payment reminder, shortfall warning).
  - **Credit limit alert:** with a limit set, the card can alert at a percentage of it (10–95% in steps of 5,
    or off). What counts is everything owed on the card, billed or not. Crossing it sends one notification
    (again only after it drops back below), warns in Quick Add, and shows on Summary, Accounts and the card.
  - Accounts shows the closed statement due ("¥42,800 · Due Dec 2") and "Next bill so far ¥9,860".
  - Starting values (card setup, also for a card nothing has been logged on yet): the unpaid statement
    goes on the closed bill; "spent since" the last closing day goes on the bill that's building, as an
    adjustment dated the day the card was added.
- A purchase with Olive in debit mode is recorded on SMBC bank, not on Olive credit.
- **Order and grouping.** Accounts appear in one custom order (`Account.sortOrder`, ties by creation date).
  Accounts tab → **Edit** (or Settings → Reorder accounts) drags them into place. Every account picker
  uses the same order, with credit cards first.
  - **Group accounts by type** (Settings, also in the Edit sheet; `groupAccountsByType`, on by default since the canvas redesign)
    splits the non-credit list into Bank accounts, Prepaid wallets and Cash, in that fixed order, each with
    its total. Off shows one "Money" list. Credit cards are always their own list.
  - When grouped, you drag within a group. Reordering renumbers everything to match the screen, so the
    flat list then follows the grouped order (types stay together until you drag them apart again).
  - Archived accounts aren't reorderable and stay at the bottom. New accounts go last.

## Entry types

| Type | Counts as spending? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expense | Yes, my share only | The full amount leaves the account. The refund flag reduces spending instead. |
| Income | No; counted as income | Salary; Dad (iPhone share), which is a gift. |
| Transfer | No | Between my own accounts, including top-ups. A top-up into PASMO counts as Transport. |
| Card payoff | No | Bank → credit card. Has a **Confirmed** tick; unconfirmed payoffs still move money. |
| Repayment (settlement) | No, and not income | Partner pays me back. Increases the receiving account. |
| Balance adjustment | No | Signed correction. |

## Card bills

- Payment dates move off weekends and Japanese bank holidays per the card's rule (`JapaneseHolidays`:
  national holidays, 振替休日, 国民の休日, the 2019–2021 one-off moves, and the banks' Dec 31 – Jan 3
  closure). Sun Dec 27 → Mon Dec 28; Sat Jan 2 → Mon Jan 4. Screens say why ("the 27th is a Sunday").
- On the actual (shifted) payment day, a **Card payoff** for the due amount is recorded automatically as a
  transfer from the paying account. It's editable, starts unconfirmed and shows an **Auto** badge. Paidy
  only gets one if it has a paid-from account.
- Each cycle is processed once, so deleting an automatic payoff doesn't bring it back.
- A bill shows up as soon as its statement closes. Kakeibo projects the paying account's balance on the due
  date, counting future-dated entries (scheduled income) and earlier bills from the same account. If it
  falls short, Summary, the card and the account show the shortfall with a one-tap transfer from the money
  account with the most in it (`Billing.suggestedSource`).
- Notifications (local, `BillNotifications`): when a statement closes, "statement closed" or "bill not
  covered"; for a bill that won't be covered, 9:00 three days and one day before; for any other bill, 9:00
  three days before. "Card paid" when a payoff is recorded automatically. Global switches in Settings,
  per-card switches in card setup.
- Activity shows "statement closed" rows on the day each statement closes.

## Card installments (分割払い)

- A card purchase can be paid in installments: in Quick Add, ⋯ › **Pay in installments…** (only for an
  expense on a credit card; also when editing, for あとから分割). You enter the total and pick the months
  (2, 3, 5, 6, 10, 12, 15, 18, 20, 24, 30, 36 or a custom count). The remainder of the split goes on the
  first installment (¥100,000 over 3 → 33,334 + 33,333 + 33,333).
- Installment 1 is on the bill the purchase would normally land on; the rest follow monthly, moved off
  weekends and holidays like any bill. Statements bill only that month's installment; auto payoffs,
  shortfall warnings and bank forecasts follow.
- How it counts, chosen per purchase: **Monthly** (default; each installment in the month it's paid),
  **All now** (the full price in the purchase month) or **Debt only** (never budget spending; it only lowers
  net worth). A partner's share is owed in full at once.
- Fees: each card has an optional annual rate (`installmentFeeRate`); the fee is worked out with level
  payments (元利均等), which matches Japanese fee tables (3 payments at 15% = ¥2.51 per ¥100). Any purchase
  can set an exact fee instead (¥0 for interest-free campaigns). Two installments are always free. Fees are
  charged a slice on each statement and count as spending in **Card fees**, created when first needed.
- The whole unpaid price counts against the credit limit and the limit alert, and in "Owed on cards".
- Installments billed before the card was added to Kakeibo count as already paid.
- The plan can be changed until its first statement closes; after that only **Pay off the rest** (the
  installments not yet billed move onto the open bill, without further fees) or deleting the purchase.
- Shown as: one Activity row with a "24 months" badge; this month's slices on the Category page; an
  Installments card and timeline on the purchase; an Installments list and the installments on the current
  bill on the card page.
- Not handled: refunds of installment purchases (pay off the rest, then log the refund), notifications when
  a plan ends.

## Partner ("paid for both of us")

- Split options on an expense: 50% (default when splitting), all hers, or a custom yen amount. Only my
  share counts as spending.
- One running total shows what she owes: her shares minus her repayments.
- **Settle up** takes any amount and applies it to the oldest items first. **Mark repaid** on one item
  creates a repayment aimed at that item.
- When she pays for something shared, I log my share as a normal expense. There is no reverse balance for now.
- The "Girlfriend repayments" income category was dropped: repayments aren't income.

## Categories and budgets

- Expense categories: Rent, Utilities, Phone, Transport, Supermarket, Konbini, Restaurants, Shopping,
  Subscriptions, Other.
- Income categories: Salary, Dad (iPhone share).
- Each category can have one fixed monthly budget (optional). Unspent budget doesn't roll over.
- The **Budgets** tab sorts categories with a budget by attention (see Budget pace below) and lists
  categories without one, with their spending this month, under **No budget set** (archived categories
  included when they have spending). Totals, the split bar and trends always include all spending.
  Settings → Categories & budgets lists every category.
- Categories can be renamed, reordered and given an emoji (shown when no SF Symbol matches the name).
  They are archived, never deleted.
- Months are calendar months. The start day is stored as a setting (`monthStartDay`) for later.

## Entry flow (Quick Add)

- The + in the navigation bar of Summary, Activity and Budgets opens Quick Add with the keypad live: amount → category → **Save**. Holding +
  offers Expense, Income, Transfer and the last three different expenses to log again.
- Category and account are pre-picked from history (`Suggestions`: time of day, weekday or weekend,
  recency; the account last used with that category), unless "Suggest category & account" is off. The
  grid shows the seven most used categories in your own order, plus All.
- Layout, top to bottom: close, Expense/Income/Transfer, ⋯ (split, refund, delete); the amount (income in
  green with +¥); quick-entry chips (new expenses only); date/time and note chips; "Paid with" account chips; category tiles; the keypad pinned
  to the bottom with 00, 000 and a tall Save key. The note's keyboard covers the pad rather than squashing
  the screen.
- Save closes the sheet. A new entry then shows "Saved ¥980 · Dining Out · Wallet" with **Undo** over the
  app for a few seconds (never a dialog). The canvas kept the sheet open for logging several in a row; in
  use, closing it was wanted.
- Transfer shows each account's balance before and after, with a swap button.
- Other ways in: the widgets, the Control Center control / Action Button ("Log Expense"), and the "Add
  Kakeibo Entry" shortcut.
- **Log Payment** intent (amount, merchant, card): saves an expense in the background.
  - The account is matched from the Wallet card name; the category from the last entry at the same merchant.
  - It's marked "to review". It's ready for a Wallet transaction automation, which hasn't been tested on
    Japanese cards (iD/QUICPay may not pass the merchant).

## Quick entries

For purchases that are the same every time (daily breakfast, a usual coffee). Not repeating entries (see
Later): nothing is logged on a schedule; you log it when it happens.

- A **quick entry** (`QuickEntry`) stores a name, amount, category, account (empty means the default
  account), optional merchant, optional partner split and an emoji (empty means the category's). Expenses
  only.
- Managed in Settings › Organize › **Quick entries** (add, edit, reorder, swipe to delete), or made from an
  existing expense: edit it, then ⋯ › **Save as Quick Entry…** fills in the editor from that entry.
- The logged entry is an ordinary expense: note = the quick entry's name, merchant = its merchant, not
  marked "to review". It remembers its quick entry (`Entry.quickEntryID`) for the widget's count.
- If its category or account is archived it still logs, and the Settings list shows an orange note. If its
  account was deleted, the default account is used.
- **Quick Add:** a row of chips above the date and note. A tap fills in amount, category, account, split,
  note and merchant (the date stays as it is), and **Save** logs it. Anything can still be changed first.
- **Quick entries widget** (Home Screen and the swipe-right Today View): small 2 buttons, medium 4, large 8,
  in the Settings order. The widget can't open the database, so:
  - The app publishes a copy of each quick entry and today's counts to the App Group (`QuickEntryStore`)
    every time `Maintenance.run` runs.
  - A tap adds a copy of the quick entry plus the tap time to a pending list in the App Group. The button
    flashes ✓ and shows **today ×N** (the app's count plus unsaved taps), so a double tap is visible. There's
    no undo on the widget; delete a mistake in the app.
  - The app saves pending taps as entries when it opens or comes to the foreground, dated when they were
    tapped. The tap's ID becomes the entry's ID, so the same list can't be saved twice. No haptic, toast
    or budget warning for these. Until then the taps aren't in balances or totals.
  - If the installer drops the App Group, the widget has no quick entries to show, so taps can't go
    missing unnoticed. With none set up it says "Add quick entries in Kakeibo" and opens
    `kakeibo://quick-entries`.
- **Shortcuts:** "Log Quick Entry" (pick one) saves it straight away without opening the app, e.g. from the
  Action Button or a time-of-day automation.
- Quick entries are in the JSON backup. Restoring a backup from before they existed leaves the current
  ones alone.

## Widgets

- Besides Quick entries (above), three Home Screen widgets show numbers: **Budget** (small, medium, Lock
  Screen), **Next card bill** (small) and **Balances** (small, medium). The widget process can't open the database, so everything they show comes
  from the `WidgetSnapshot` the app writes after every save and whenever it opens. Balances are therefore
  as of the last time the app saved or was opened: a future-dated entry or automatic payoff whose date
  arrives while the app is closed shows up the next time it opens.
- **Balances** is a glance at a few accounts, not an overview: at most four, no total.
  - Accounts are chosen per widget (touch and hold → Edit Widget), in the order they're dragged into
    there, so two widgets can show different groups. Any account that tracks a balance can be chosen,
    credit cards included; top-ups that count as spending have no balance to show.
  - A widget that was never edited shows the first four accounts in the app's order. Once edited, the
    list is explicit: new accounts stay out until added. Archived and deleted accounts drop out without
    another taking their place; an unarchived one comes back. An emptied list says "Choose accounts".
  - Each account shows its symbol (or card colour), name and the full amount, never the short form; the
    name truncates first. Cards show what's owed as a positive number, as on the Accounts tab; a negative
    bank or cash balance is red. With fewer than four accounts the rest grow to fill the widget.
  - Medium: each account opens its own page (`kakeibo://account/<id>`). Small widgets get one tap target
    from iOS, so it opens the Accounts tab (`kakeibo://accounts`).
- Settings → "Hide widget amounts when locked" (off by default) marks the amounts in those three widgets
  privacy-sensitive, so iOS blanks them while the iPhone is locked.

## Haptics

Used often but never on plain navigation, scrolling or opening a sheet. iOS's System Haptics switch turns
them all off; Settings › Keypad haptics silences only the keypad. Every tap is a full-strength impact (the
soft, low-intensity ones were too faint to feel). Generators are kept warm (`Haptics` in `Components.swift`;
`.sensoryFeedback(.firmSelection, …)` in views).

| Moment | Feel |
| --- | --- |
| Number-pad key (unless Settings › Keypad haptics is off) | Firm medium tap |
| Key that does nothing (backspace on ¥0, digit limit) | Heavy thud |
| Type, split, refund, account, category or date changed; month arrows; filters; toggles; Undo | Rigid tap |
| Entry saved; settle up; mark repaid; payoff confirmed or recorded; balance adjusted; backup restored | Success |
| Entry saved but its category is now over budget (my share, entry's month) | Warning |
| Entry deleted | Warning |
| Dropping a reordered account or category | Heavy tap |

## Data and privacy

- Settings offers a JSON backup ("Back Up Now", with the date of the last one; includes quick entries), restore, and a CSV export of
  transactions, all via the share sheet or Files. Backups aren't encrypted yet.
- Optional Face ID (or passcode) lock when the app opens or comes back; a cover hides amounts in the app
  switcher (on by default). Widget amounts can be hidden while the iPhone is locked (off by default).
- The schema is CloudKit-compatible: every field has a default, nothing is marked unique, and links are
  UUIDs rather than relationships.

## Later

- Repeating and scheduled entries (rent, phone, salary): the canvas shows a Repeat row and scheduled salary
  in a bank account's Upcoming list. Future-dated entries already count in projections.
- Moving budget between categories for one month ("Cover the ¥1,800 · Move from Shopping"): needs
  per-month budget overrides. Only "Raise budget" is built.
- Notification actions (Transfer, Remind Me Tomorrow), receipt photos, encrypted backups, the card's
  billing-calendar chart and statement reconciliation ("Matches your entries").
- Payday-based months (`monthStartDay` is stored) and the week start setting.
- Testing the Wallet transaction automation on SMBC (Visa and iD), Rakuten (QUICPay), Revolut and Suica/PASMO.
- CSV import: Revolut first, then a column-mapping importer with Shift_JIS detection and duplicate matching.
- iCloud sync (needs a paid developer account) and sharing a joint account with my partner via CloudKit
  sharing (`Account.shareScope` is reserved for this).
- Other currencies (`currencyCode` exists on accounts and entries).

## Look and layout (canvas redesign, v2)

The spec is the Claude Design canvas "Kakeibo"; tokens are recorded in CLAUDE.md and defined in
`Shared/DesignTokens.swift`.

- Dark mode in the iOS 26 language (the canvas's values); light mode follows the phone with derived colours
  that keep 4.5:1. Indigo (藍) is the accent: one prominent action per surface. Green means income, on
  track or covered; red over budget; orange a shortfall, at risk or spent more; blue spent less.
- Categories are rounded squares, accounts circles, credit cards small cards in their own colour; glyphs
  are SF Symbols matched on the category name. Unknown categories keep their emoji.
- Tabs: Summary, Activity, Budgets, Accounts in the system tab bar (Liquid Glass on iOS 26, shrinking while
  scrolling down). The custom floating bar with + beside it was dropped: content could end up behind it at the
  bottom of a page, and it lacked the system's glass selection. + sits in the navigation bar instead (tap for
  an expense; hold for income, a transfer or a recent entry).
- **Summary**: a bill that won't be covered (with Transfer and View bill), this month's budget with what's
  left, the daily allowance for the days after today, today's pace tick, the status and a like-for-like
  comparison with the same days last month; Needs attention; Upcoming card bills; Recent. The partner
  balance and entries to review appear in a "To do" card only when there's something to do. The old
  spending chart, quick actions and Edit Home are gone (`homeSectionOrder` and `homeHiddenSections` are
  no longer read).
- **Activity**: search, type/account/category filters, months and days with totals, swipe to duplicate or
  delete, statement-closed rows. Rows open a **Transaction** page with the card-bill timeline and what the
  entry did to its budget.
- **Budgets**: This Month (overview with the category split bar and pace; Needs attention, On track, No
  budget set) and Trends (spending so far against last month and the recent average, monthly totals with a
  projection, category changes over the same days, what was kept last month). Categories open a
  **Category** page.
- **Budget pace**: today's pace = budget × days elapsed ÷ days in the month. Categories sort over budget,
  then likely to run out (projected from this month's daily rate, with the date), then on track. A category
  exactly used up (rent) isn't "at risk". Comparisons are like-for-like: Nov 1–18 against Oct 1–18.
- **Accounts**: net worth; bank accounts, prepaid wallets and cash with totals and one status line each;
  credit cards. A bank account page shows the last and next four weeks, upcoming items with the balance
  after, and history with a running balance. A card page shows the payment warning, the current bill, the
  bill building now and past statements.
